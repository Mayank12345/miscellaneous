For the Ashtavakra Gita bedtime story

Bedtime Narrator Test

Open-source voices, women's and men's, read the same opening lines at the same volume. The names are hidden so you judge by ear alone. Listen, pick the one you would want to fall asleep to, then reveal the names.

What every voice reads (step 1 of the story)Once upon a time, there was a little boy whose body was bent in eight places. Some people laughed at him. But he was the wisest person in the whole kingdom. Here's a question. Is a bendy banana any less yummy than a straight one?
Tip: use headphones, and listen at bedtime volume.

About this test

Every clip says the words correctly.Each one was transcribed by an independent speech-recognition model (Whisper) and matched the script word for word.
Voices that copy another voice.Chatterbox and Fish Audio copy the voice of a short sample recording. To avoid copying any real person, each was given a synthetic Kokoro voice as its sample, and brings its own rhythm and expression to it.
About the Fish Audio voices.Fish Audio S2 Pro takes spoken-style instructions inside the text, such as "soft, slow, gentle bedtime storyteller" or "warm, deep, unhurried audiobook narrator". It chooses its own pause lengths, so its clips are shorter; the other voices have fixed silences added. Its license allows free personal and non-commercial use only.
